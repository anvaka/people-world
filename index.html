About

This website was created by @anvaka. It takes the graph of the world's land borders and draws a made-up world from it.

Three rules hold on every map: neighbours share a border, countries that are not neighbours never touch, and every country is as large as its population.

Click a country to see its neighbours. Remove or add a border, and the world is drawn again. You can also drop a Graphviz .dot file onto this page to see your own graph as a map.

You can find the entire source code here. If you love this website you can also buy me a coffee or sponsor me on GitHub, but you don't have to.

I hope you like it! Please let me know.
